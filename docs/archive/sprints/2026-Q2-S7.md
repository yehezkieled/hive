**Status:** ✅ Closed 2026-06-21 — goal met (Phase 3 complete; named-library track retired). Frozen.

**Goal:** Finish Phase 3 by shipping **Track 2** — the named interaction-pattern
library that gives Team Leads a reusable vocabulary for structuring Workflow
fan-outs. S5/S6 proved the Lead→Workflow engine and hardened the maestro loop,
but a Lead still authors every fan-out's coordination shape from scratch
(free-form, no template). S7 fixes **how** a pattern is delivered to a Lead
(034, ADR) and ships all three named patterns on it — **`debate`** (034),
**`blackboard`** (035), **`tournament`** (036) — completing Phase 3. In
parallel, on independent surfaces, S7 closes two loop-correctness gaps: **032**
(validate entity/team names before they reach git/worktree paths) and **033**
(tell the PA Maestro it *is* the PA — its prompt never states the structural role
024's write-policy already enforces). The web/PWA "iPad daily driver" (Phase 4)
follows in **S8**.
