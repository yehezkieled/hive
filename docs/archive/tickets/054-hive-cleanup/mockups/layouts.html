C · Two-lane (inbox + workspace)

A persistent needs-you rail on the left, the active project's workspace on the right. Feels like an inbox next to a desk. Shines in landscape / on a laptop; tighter on portrait.

My recommendation: A (Stack) as the iPad home — the purest "default calm, exceptions loud", portrait-first — and tapping a project opens B (Focus) for a heads-down look at one build. So: "A that opens into B." Pick one, or tell me your blend.
