about this mockup
A design prototype for ticket 053 (tabbed maestro Work view), part of the Delegator's Desk redesign. The A/B toggle up top decides one thing: A keeps Home and the Work view on one page — switching is an instant client-side toggle, so the live stream, your open tabs and unsent drafts survive. B makes the Work view a separate route — every tap is a full page load (white flash, stream reconnects, extra tabs + drafts dropped). Flip it, open a couple of tabs, type a draft, bounce Home and back — that's the cost you're weighing.
